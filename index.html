Khaleja[a] (transl. Courage)[3] is a 2010 Indian Telugu-language fantasy
action comedy film written and directed by Trivikram Srinivas. It stars
Mahesh Babu and Anushka Shetty, alongside Prakash Raj, Rao Ramesh, Shafi,
Sunil, Ali, and Subbaraju in supporting roles.
